Selected work

VBR Lab

A live-streaming simulator that tests whether a video player should trust a stream’s nominal bitrate or the real size of its next few segments. It started from a question Netflix’s live team raised about capped VBR, and it runs in your browser.

JavaScript and SVG, no dependencies, unit-tested
